UNDERGRADUATE THESIS Featured Project

ALERSENSE: Design of Wearable Physiological Response Sensors and YOLOv12-Based Attention Monitoring with Haptic Feedback

A wearable attention-monitoring system that detects student attention in real time and delivers instant haptic feedback to keep learners engaged in the classroom.

An intelligent multimodal attention detection and classroom management system designed to support teachers and students with fast, accurate, and non-invasive biosensing tools. Built with a commitment to practical classroom deployment and data-driven education, ALERSENSE empowers teachers to monitor student attention levels in real time by combining wearable physiological sensors that track Heart Rate, Galvanic Skin Response, and Skin Temperature with a YOLOv12-powered camera module that recognizes inattentive behaviors. When both systems simultaneously confirm inattention, the student's wearable delivers a gentle haptic vibration, while the teacher's live dashboard updates instantly, bridging the gap between biometric data and meaningful classroom intervention.
